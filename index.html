How to play

ROLL ROLL BALL is played with one 52-card deck, shuffled fresh every round. Press Ready, drop chips on the bets you want, then press Deal.

The three cards. The 1st card decides SMALL (A to 6) or BIG (8 to K). The 2nd card decides ODDS (A, 3, 5, 9, J, K) or EVEN (2, 4, 6, 8, 10, Q). The 3rd card decides RED (hearts, diamonds) or BLACK (spades, clubs). All three pay 1:1. A seven is neither small nor big, neither odds nor even, and neither red nor black: those 1:1 bets simply stay and your stake comes back. On the wheel a seven does belong to a side by its suit: a seven of hearts or clubs counts as small, spades or diamonds as big (1st card); hearts or spades count as odds, diamonds or clubs as even (2nd card); and the 3rd card is red or black by its colour.

The sevens. If the 3rd card is a seven, ANY SEVEN pays 11:1, and the matching seven (hearts, spades, diamonds or crosses) pays 50:1.

The wheel. The three answers (small or big, odds or even, red or black) point to one of 8 sectors. Each sector holds 6 cards, 3 values in two suits, for example Small Odds Red holds the aces, 3s and 5s of hearts and diamonds. The four sevens are the white spokes between the sectors.

Winning number. After the 3rd card the dealer turns more cards, one at a time, until a card from the winning sector shows. If that is card number n, its value is the winning number. A bet on that value pays 2n minus 1 to 1 (the later it comes, the more it pays). A bet on either of the other two values in the winning sector pays 2:1. Other sectors lose.

Roll or No Roll. The ball sits on last round’s 3rd card. When the new round starts, the ball ROLLS if the new 1st card shares the size (small, big), the odds or even, or the colour with it. It is NO ROLL when all three are different. The board shows the roll and no-roll streaks.

Roll bets are patterns counted from the next round: 1, 2 or 3 rolls followed by a no roll, two no rolls in a row (DOUBLE NO ROLL), or 4 or 5 rolls in a row. They stay on the table, you pay once, until they win or the pattern breaks.

Payouts are shown as X:1, which means you win X times your bet and keep your stake. The paytable lists the exact chance and the house edge of every bet; the Roll bet payouts are worked out from the exact chances and rounded down to the nearest quarter. Cash is play money with no real value.
